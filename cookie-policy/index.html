1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit websites. They help websites remember preferences, analyze performance, and deliver personalized user experiences.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Necessary Cookies: Essential for fundamental website navigation, security verification, and remembering your cookie consent status. These cannot be disabled in our systems.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure anonymous visitor metrics, such as page load speed, referral pathways, and high-traffic articles. These are only activated upon your explicit consent.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to evaluate the reach and performance of our professional announcements and sponsored thought leadership content. Activated only upon explicit consent.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can adjust or revoke your cookie choices at any time by triggering our interactive preference manager below or by clearing your browser cache.

4. Browser Controls

Most modern web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via your browser settings (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Note that disabling necessary cookies may impair site functionality.
