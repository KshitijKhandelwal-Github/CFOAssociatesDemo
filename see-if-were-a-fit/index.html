QUALIFICATION MATRIX

We partner with founder-led businesses ready for institutional financial rigor.

Fractional CFO leadership works best when there is alignment on timing, scale, and operational readiness. Before booking a strategy call, review our engagement criteria below.

You are an ideal fit if:

  • Revenue Scale: Founder-led, post-revenue scaling company generating between ₹3 Cr and ₹100 Cr (or $500K – $15M ARR).
  • Strategic Inflection: Navigating critical milestones — preparing for institutional fundraising (Seed to Series B), international expansion, or founder succession.
  • Outgrown Bookkeeping: Outgrown traditional accounting firms or statutory CAs and require proactive, forward-looking strategic financial modeling.
  • High ROI on Overhead: Need senior-level financial leadership without the ₹75L – ₹1.2 Cr+ executive overhead and recruiting risk of a full-time hire.
  • Collaborative Leadership: Committed to transparent data sharing, disciplined monthly operating cadences, and rapid executive execution.

We are not the right fit if:

  • Pre-revenue or idea-stage concepts seeking volunteer advisory or pure equity-only compensation.
  • Looking solely for basic commodity tax filing, routine data entry, or statutory bookkeeper services without strategic CFO needs.
  • Firms unwilling to grant leadership visibility into financials, unit economics, banking, and strategic planning.
  • Organizations seeking shortcuts, dubious tax schemes, or aggressive non-compliant financial practices.

What happens next:

1. Submit Assessment Takes ~2 minutes. Tells us your current revenue tier, accounting setup, and primary financial objective.
↓
2. 30-Minute Confidential Discovery Direct 1-on-1 call with a senior CFO partner. No sales reps, no pitching — just an objective evaluation of your numbers.
↓
3. Tailored 90-Day Roadmap If aligned, we present a structured engagement scope, SLA deliverables, and embed into your team within 5–7 business days.
