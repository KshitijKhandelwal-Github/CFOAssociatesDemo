FAQ

Who we work with: common questions

Is a fractional CFO worth it for a company doing $2M to $5M in revenue?
Yes, if the financial complexity of your business has outpaced what a founder and basic bookkeeper can manage. Clear indicators: you cannot calculate product-level gross margin in under 60 seconds, you are burning through runway faster than forecast, or you are preparing to raise capital. If revenue is still unpredictable or books are trivial, a full CFO is premature; fractional bridges this window with maximum ROI.
Do tech and SaaS companies require a fractional or virtual CFO?
Post-revenue scaling tech and SaaS businesses between $2M and $50M in ARR almost always benefit from fractional leadership. Your finance function must support investor decks, ASC 606 revenue recognition, international tax corridors, ESOP plans, and fundraising diligence. A founder doing this solo becomes the ultimate operational bottleneck.
Can you work with non-tech or manufacturing businesses?
Absolutely. In fact, the stakes of getting working capital, debt covenants, and product-level margins wrong in physical or manufacturing businesses are immediate. We have guided manufacturing, logistics, and retail founders on inventory turns, commercial credit negotiations, capex planning, and transaction advisory.
What types of companies do you NOT work with?
We do not take on pre-revenue concept-stage startups (too early for CFO horsepower), public corporations with $100M+ revenue (who require a full-time 40-hour executive team), or situations where founders are passive and absent from operations. We partner with active operators building high-growth enterprises.
