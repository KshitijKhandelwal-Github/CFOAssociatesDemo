FAQ

Fractional CFO services: common questions

What does a fractional CFO do for a SaaS or tech company?
For SaaS and tech businesses, fractional CFO work centers on unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback, gross margin by product), ARR accounting and revenue recognition under ASC 606, cash runway modeling, investor reporting, and fundraising. CFOAssociates embeds veteran former SaaS CFOs who ensure your numbers are investor-grade and that your financial infrastructure scales seamlessly through your next milestone.
When should a founder hire a fractional CFO instead of a full-time one?
Hire fractional when you need strategic, executive financial leadership but your business does not require a 40-hour weekly presence. This is typical for founder-led companies between M and M in revenue. A full-time CFO often costs K–K+ in salary, bonus, and equity. Fractional gives you veteran operator caliber at a fraction of the cost, on flexible month-to-month terms.
What is the difference between a fractional CFO, virtual CFO, and interim CFO?
They represent the same high operator caliber with different operational modalities. A fractional CFO works part-time on an ongoing basis. A virtual CFO delivers the same fractional scope optimized for remote and distributed teams. An interim CFO provides high-capacity, full-time coverage to bridge a finite gap, such as a departing CFO or an active M&A process.
Do you replace our existing bookkeeper or CPA firm?
No — we elevate them. Bookkeepers record transactions; CPAs handle year-end tax returns. A CFO sits between your accounting foundation and your executive leadership, interpreting the past to build forward-looking strategy, manage cash flow, present to the board, and steer fundraising.
What is transaction advisory and when do I need it?
Transaction advisory is specialized financial guidance for mergers, acquisitions, and recapitalizations. On the sell side, we defend your valuation, build the data room, and manage working capital peg true-ups. On the buy side, we conduct quality of earnings due diligence. You need transaction advisory the moment a strategic deal discussion begins, not after term sheets are finalized.
How quickly can CFOAssociates get started and deliver results?
We can begin onboarding within 7 business days of signing our mutual NDA and agreement. Week 1 is dedicated to systems access, historical data audit, and stakeholder alignment. By day 30, your 13-week cash model, board reporting package, and prioritized financial initiatives are live.
