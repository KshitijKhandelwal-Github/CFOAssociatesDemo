JOIN THE PRACTICE BENCH

Do the best financial work of your life, alongside ambitious founders.

We are building the premier fractional CFO and finance leadership practice in India and Southeast Asia. We look for seasoned finance directors, former CFOs, and analytical powerhouses who prefer high-impact execution over corporate bureaucracy.

📍 Work Style: Hybrid & Remote-First • Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Singapore

HOW WE WORK

Built for high-caliber finance operators.

Operator Mindset Over Theory

We do not write passive slide decks or deliver vague high-level recommendations. We build the cash flow models, reconcile the unit economics, and sit across the table with institutional investors alongside the founder.

High Autonomy, Zero Bureaucracy

No arbitrary timesheet policing or pointless status meetings. You own the financial relationship with your portfolio clients, backed by our centralized modeling frameworks, research bench, and partner review network.

Multi-Industry Cross-Pollination

Instead of being trapped in one company's annual cycle, you engage with 3–4 high-growth businesses across SaaS, D2C, fintech, and logistics — accelerating your operating experience tenfold.
