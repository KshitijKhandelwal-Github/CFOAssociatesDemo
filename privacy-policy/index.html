Privacy Policy

How we collect, protect, and handle your data and proprietary business information.

1. Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

At CFOAssociates ("[Legal Company Name]", "we", "us", or "our"), we understand that financial, operational, and corporate data is the lifeblood of founder-led companies. We treat all information submitted through our website and client portals with the highest degree of confidentiality and data security.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following circumstances:

  • Inquiry & Contact Information: When you submit an inquiry form or schedule a discovery call, we collect your name, business email address, company name, phone number, and revenue stage.
  • Usage & Analytics Data: With your consent via our Cookie Preferences banner, we collect anonymized analytics data regarding website navigation, browser type, and referrer URLs to improve our service offerings.
  • Client Financial Information: Under separately executed Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), clients may provide access to financial statements, accounting databases, cap tables, and board presentations. This data is strictly governed by the client engagement agreement and not covered by general website telemetry.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly to:

  • Evaluate and respond to discovery call requests and inbound inquiries.
  • Prepare customized service proposals and engagement scopes.
  • Deliver insights, guides, and newsletters if you have explicitly subscribed.
  • Maintain website security, verify consent records, and satisfy legal compliance.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal or company information. Information is only shared with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our digital infrastructure (such as secure cloud hosting, form processing, and encrypted email services), all bound by strict confidentiality covenants.

5. Security Standards

We deploy industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL in transit, AES-256 at rest) and strict access controls across all communication channels. Client financial files are managed exclusively within dedicated, access-controlled virtual data rooms.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA, or applicable data protection regulations), you retain the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may update your cookie preferences at any time using the preferences link in our footer.
